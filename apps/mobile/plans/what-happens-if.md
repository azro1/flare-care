## Product shape

- Compact **section / card** (not a giant emergency product).  
- **Who I contact** — user-authored (fits My IBD team / self-advocacy: they enter their IBD nurse / clinic / out-of-hours path).  
- **When I should seek help** — **clinically reviewed** guidance only (not AI-generated, not FlareCare inventing red-flag rules).  
- Likely Support or Care-adjacent; could also surface from IBD at work / Going Out as “if things worsen.”

---

## Guardrails

- Escalation / red-flag copy must be **clinically reviewed** (and kept up to date) — lawsuit / harm risk if we freestyle medical thresholds.  
- Contact details: **user-entered** (same rule as My IBD — no pulling records).  
- Clear framing: guidance to help decide when to seek care — **not** a diagnosis tool, not a substitute for emergency services.  
- Prefer linking or quoting known good sources / clinician-approved text over inventing symptoms lists in-app.
