# Plan: Private Support topics (sensitive IBD life)

**Status:** Idea — not built. Massive support gap; handle carefully. **Support only** — never a dashboard tile.

**Doc home:** `apps/mobile/plans/`. Short backlog pointer also under **Looking ahead** in `DEV_NOTES.md` / `FEATURES.md`.

---

## Why it matters

The embarrassing stuff people don’t talk about. A 2025 patient/caregiver study found people talking about:

- sex lives  
- relationships  
- embarrassment  
- social avoidance  
- difficulty explaining the condition to others  

There are also substantial information gaps around **fertility, pregnancy, and sexual health**. One survey found only **29.3%** of patients had good/very good pregnancy-specific knowledge, while **63%** reported receiving **no** information about IBD and pregnancy.

Huge support gap — but don’t rush it.

---

## Product shape

**Do not** build a “Sex & Relationships” feature into the dashboard.

Put **private, optional resources inside Support**, under something like **Private topics**:

- Sex & relationships  
- Fertility & pregnancy  
- Body image  
- Talking to others about IBD  

---

## Guardrails

- Optional / discreet entry (Support hub), not Home shelves.  
- Educational / supportive resources — not clinical advice or diagnosis.  
- Tone: respectful, non-clinical where possible; clear “not medical advice” where needed.  
- Fits FlareCare philosophy: everyday living gaps apps ignore — not platform completeness.
